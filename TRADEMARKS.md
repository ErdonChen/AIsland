# AIsland Brand and Trademark Policy

The Apache License 2.0 applies to AIsland Community Edition source code. It does not grant permission to use the AIsland name, logo, or official release identity beyond reasonable factual reference to the project's origin.

## Allowed without additional permission

- Refer to the project as “AIsland” when discussing or linking to the official project.
- State that modified software is “based on AIsland” when the statement is accurate and does not imply endorsement.
- Redistribute an unmodified official source archive while preserving its license, notices, and origin.

## Permission required

- Publish a modified build using the AIsland name or logo as its product identity.
- Present a fork, installer, service, domain, social account, or paid offering as official, approved, certified, or supported by AIsland.
- Use confusingly similar branding that may cause users to mistake a third-party build for an official release.

Modified distributions must use another product name and visual identity and must clearly identify their changes. Questions about brand use may be sent to `aisland_support@163.com`.

This policy describes project permission and is not a statement that a trademark registration has been completed in every jurisdiction.
