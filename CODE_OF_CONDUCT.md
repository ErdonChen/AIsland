# AIsland Code of Conduct

AIsland adopts the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/) in full for all project spaces, including Issues, Pull Requests, reviews, discussions, and occasions where someone officially represents the project.

Participants must communicate honestly and respectfully, accept constructive feedback, protect other people's private information, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, or sustained disruption.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `aisland_support@163.com`. Do not open a public Issue containing a complaint or another person's private information. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical.

Depending on impact and history, the maintainer may correct or remove content, issue a private warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. The maintainer will consider context, proportionality, safety, and opportunities to repair harm.

Security vulnerabilities must use [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/ErdonChen/AIsland/security/advisories/new), not the conduct channel.
